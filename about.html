Meet theTough Tigers

A small, independent shop opened by two barbers who love the classics.

The barbers

Ukrainian-born Alina worked as a barber in cities around the world before she and Maks settled in Winnipeg and opened Tough Tiger together.

Alina

aka Queen Rockabilly

Specialises in traditional American classic haircuts of the 1950s: pompadours, side parts, flattops. Uppercut Deluxe named her one of their ambassadors. Clients call her calm, precise and honest about what will suit them.

Maks

fades and beards

Known for skin fades that are never rushed and beard work finished with hot and cold towels and a straight razor. Trusted with pre-wedding grooming, too.

Inside the shop

Tough Tiger feels like a barbershop from another decade, with the comfort of this one.

Old-school rock

Classic rock and roll playing softly in the background.

Coffee on the house

Free coffee while you wait, plus cold drinks in the pink fridge.

Vintage chairs

Vintage barber chairs, a checkered floor and tattoo flash on every wall.

Cash is king

The shop motto. Ask about payment when you call to book.

Say hi to Easy

The shop French bulldog. Easy greets clients at the door, supervises from the red barber chair and poses for the odd product shoot. Regulars know him well.
