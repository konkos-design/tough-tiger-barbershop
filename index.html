Two barbers. One old-school shop.

Tough Tiger is run by Alina and Maks, a husband-and-wife team. Alina, known online as Queen Rockabilly, specialises in the classic American haircuts of the 1950s and is an ambassador for Uppercut Deluxe. Maks is the one to see for sharp fades and detailed beard work.

  • Vintage barber chairs, checkered floor and tattoo flash on the walls
  • Free coffee and a fridge with cold drinks
  • Easy the French bulldog on welcome duty
